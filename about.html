Who

All my life I have been drawn to self-improvement, immersion, motivation, brain science, and how learning works, and I have read the books.

As a child I was forced to study and met violence, and after that I became a child who refused to learn on purpose.

So my grades were bad for all of my school years, but the craving to learn never stopped.

As an adult and up to now I have loved games enough to spend a very large part of my time on them.

And after all those books I had still put no value into the world, because I never intended to. Because I was afraid.

I have now compressed a lifetime of those books, the ideas they produced in me, my experience and my opinions, into one book.

But if I can actually give people value and help them reach the result they want, then selling this book, and the course, will be the most important work of my life.
