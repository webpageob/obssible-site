The experiment

The problem

The single most important problem that has to be solved right now.

The state

Body and mind kept relaxed — leaning back in the chair. If I get sleepy, I am allowed to sleep.

Blocked

Everything that interrupts me. Everything that turns my thoughts and my attention somewhere else.

What I publish

If you produce nothing: publish every time you produce. If you already produce well: polish it a lot, then publish.

Why in public

Publishing in public creates enormous motivation. It puts me in a state where I have to do it. Add the 5-second rule — the body moves on the count of one (Mel Robbins) — and the first-five-minutes rule — focus for about five minutes, start, and it carries itself — and it becomes easy to move toward producing.

The book

You Only Know What You Make

Why we know everything and do nothing.

People read a great deal and do not change. They consumed without a purpose, so nothing was stored in the first place. Install the purpose of making something to sell, and it changes.

In progress. No date.

Who

All my life I have been drawn to self-improvement, immersion, motivation, brain science, and how learning works, and I have read the books.

And after all those books I had still put no value into the world, because I never intended to. Because I was afraid.

I have now compressed a lifetime of those books, the ideas they produced in me, my experience and my opinions, into one book.
